Generic sample `.jot` documents (made-up writing, nobody's real notes) for `test-jots.mjs` and for trying Jot pages:

    KW_JOTS=jots-sample node render.mjs month 2026-10 test.ics

`inkdeck/` looks like the files InkDeck saves on its SD card. Your own writing goes in `private/jots/` (ignored by git), or point
`KW_JOTS` at a copy of the card.
