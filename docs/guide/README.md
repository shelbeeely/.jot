# Jot guides

- [Writing in Jot](writing-jot.md): how to write a `.jot` journal by hand: dates, tasks, notes, ratings. Ten minutes; the first
  section is enough to start.
- [Using the template editor](template-editor.md): design a journal's shape and download it as a `.jot` file to fill in, on a
  computer, a phone or InkDeck.
  Its [Numbers and charts](template-editor.md#numbers-and-charts) section covers the weekly dashboard: numbers each day, a chart of
  the week.
- [Automation and connections](automation.md): put your events on your calendar, bring in numbers from a fitness app, carry tasks
  forward, and connect to other services (webhooks, Todoist, the weather), only when you ask.
- [Write your own manual](manual.md): one page that says why you keep a journal, what you do, what your marks mean and how you
  start again after a gap. The template editor writes half of it for you.

- [Voice practice](voice-practice.md): an optional pitch meter that runs on your device (nothing recorded or sent), a
  session timer and a practice note whose numbers chart over time.
- [Converting an old diary](converting.md): turn a diary kept as plain text (your own, or one from Project Gutenberg) into dated
  Jot entries; its [Your life online](converting.md#your-life-online) section turns social and blog data exports (X/Twitter,
  Mastodon, Facebook, Instagram, Tumblr, Reddit, Google Keep and Blogger) into one dated journal.
- [Turning anything into Jot](turning-anything-into-jot.md): chats, notes, meeting minutes, spreadsheets and photographed pages,
  with an AI agent's help (the to-jot skill), and `jot check` to see that a file is valid.

To see Jot at length, the [Examples](../../site/dist/examples/) page has whole public-domain diaries converted to `.jot`.

These guides are also pages on the site (`/guide/`), built from these files by `site/build-guides.mjs`. The full rules are in the
[Jot specification](../../specification/Jot-Complete-Specification.md).
