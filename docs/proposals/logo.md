# Design brief: the Jot logo

Status: done. The owner chose the iota written with a fine pen; the logo, its files and its usage guide are in
[`docs/brand/`](../brand/README.md). It replaces the hash mark (⌗) in the site header and the app icon.

**Summary.** Jot is a plain-text journal format with free tools (the website, a desktop app, a command line and a VS Code
extension) that turns things you jot down into beautiful pages, while the writing stays in a file you own.

**Audience.** Journal keepers first: diarists, planners, bullet journalers, gratitude and mood trackers, people coming back to a
notebook after a gap. Many are not technical. They meet the mark as an app icon on a phone or computer, a browser tab, the site's
header and on printed pages. Second: writers and developers who like plain text, on GitHub and in VS Code.

**Brand adjectives:** warm, handmade, calm, quiet. **Avoid feeling:** corporate or technical, loud or gamified, cute or childish,
clinical.

**Promise.** Jot things down; get beautiful pages back, and they stay yours.

**The big idea: iota.** "Jot" comes from the Greek iota (ι), the smallest letter. A jotting is a quickly written, brief note,
and jottings (snippets, scraps, a scratchpad) are a collection of them: exactly what a `.jot` file holds. The logo starts from
the iota: the smallest mark, written quickly by hand, and many of them kept together.

**Competitive landscape.** Journal and notes apps lean on a few signs: the book or notebook (Day One's bookmark ribbon,
Moleskine), the creature (Evernote's elephant, Bear's bear), the geometric tech glyph (Notion's N, Obsidian's crystal), and the
writing tool (quill, fountain-pen nib, pencil). Developer tools add the hash and the Markdown M↓. Clichés to stay away from
unless given a fresh form: an open book, spiral or ribbon notebook, quill or feather, pen nib, pencil, check mark, speech bubble,
hash sign (the current ⌗ sits close to Slack's mark). Open territory: the act of making a mark by hand, warm ink on paper, and the
smallest mark there is. The word itself helps: "jot" comes from iota, the smallest Greek letter ("not one jot").

**Mark type.** A symbol and a wordmark. The symbol must stand alone (app icon, favicon, a stamp on a notebook cover); the
wordmark is a lowercase "jot" drawn to match it, not typed in a font.

**Must work on.** A 16 px favicon; app icons for macOS, Windows, Linux, Android (adaptive) and iOS; the site header in light
(cream paper) and dark (charcoal paper); one colour in print; 1-bit on e-ink (InkDeck); a rubber stamp or emboss.

**Constraints.**

- Keep what already feels like Jot: cream paper `#fffdf6` and `#ede9df`, charcoal paper `#242622`, ink blue `#1f3a8a`, the dot
  grid and the handwritten look. One or two colours; no gradients or shadows.
- The site's handwriting fonts (Caveat, Playpen Sans, Shantell Sans, all SIL Open Font License) can inform the wordmark, but its
  letters are drawn as outlines so the logo owns them.
- Other products use the name Jot. A distinctive symbol matters more because of that; trademark clearance needs a professional
  search and is not part of this work.

**Success criteria.**

- Recognisable from its silhouette alone at 16 px.
- Drawable from memory with a pen in about five seconds: the test of a timeless mark, and fitting for a journal.
- Feels like ink from a human hand, but calm, not scribbly.
- Not mistakable for Slack, Day One, Notion or the Markdown mark side by side.
- Works as a one-colour stamp.

**Decision-maker:** the project owner. **Deliverables:** symbol, wordmark, horizontal and stacked lockups, a small-size cut,
favicon and web icons, the desktop and mobile app icons (`desktop/src-tauri/icons/`), colours, and a one-page usage guide.

**Assumptions.** The name is set lowercase, "jot". Ink blue is the brand colour, on cream. No tagline in the logo.

## Word map

- **jot** → iota (ι), the smallest letter; a dot; a tick; a quick note; "jot it down".
- **jottings** → a brief note written quickly; snippets, scraps, a scratchpad; a collection kept together.
- **writing** → ink, a line, a loop, one pen stroke, pen pressure.
- **journal** → a page, a day, coming back, keeping.
- **warm, handmade** → ink pooling at the end of a stroke, a circle that is not quite perfect, a stamp.
- **calm, quiet** → one line, a wide margin, room on the page.

Where the branches meet, to draw as concepts (the owner chose iota and jottings as the lead):

1. **Iota, dotted afterwards.** The Greek ι in one quick pen stroke, with the dot added afterwards and landing a little ahead, as
   a hand dots an i at the end of a word. Risk: a plain dotted i reads as "information"; the hooked tail and the dot's place keep
   it from that.
2. **A scrap.** A small scrap of paper (a jotting) carrying the iota: the app icon is the scrap itself.
3. **Jottings make a j.** A j built from a few short pen strokes stacked like notes on a scratchpad, with its dot: many small
   notes that together become Jot.
